## Research method and reuse

- Preserved the prior checksum-verified Geofabrik/OSM discovery batch and its provenance. Three additional club/hall identities were admitted from the same licensed extract after individual review.
- Used Nominatim for a single, cached review of existing business coordinates, plus a few exact named-address matches. One machine, one thread, no more than one request/second, identifying user agent, no runtime geocoding. Policy: https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/ . Full locality evidence: `public/data/area-reviews.json`.
- Reviewed address hierarchies and named-road/branch context. Rejected clearly wrong geocoder suggestions and retained stronger published areas. Aloki was corrected from the earlier Gulshan road-string classification to Tejgaon, corroborated by its operator’s 211 Tejgaon Link Road address. JK’s Basabo branch, Mohakhali Community Center and Tokyo Square were refined using explicit branch/address evidence.
- Operator contacts include Dhaka Ladies Club, KIB, Army Welfare Trust public hall-booking pages, Zaara, JK, X-Group’s branch list, and BCFCC. Only business/hall booking facts were used; no private schedules, staff/private contacts or security information were collected into the dataset.
- At the owner’s explicit request, manually inspected individual named Maps business listings to resolve identities and public contacts. No Places API, automated contact scraper, bulk Places export, ratings, review counts, reviews, opening hours, photos or marketing descriptions were stored. Exact listing links are retained where available, otherwise clearly formed search links remain.
- New Plus Code points were decoded with the open-source Open Location Code 1.0.1 library in temporary research tooling. No runtime dependency or service was added.
- BD Trade Info’s terms permit end-user reference only; no records or contacts were republished from that directory. Rejected the unrelated current bdwedding.com content. Access failures and robots exclusions were respected; no challenges were bypassed.
