# Initial Dhaka dataset admission

Added: 8 venues — bd-the-westin-dhaka, bd-le-meridien-dhaka, bd-sheraton-dhaka, bd-renaissance-dhaka-gulshan, bd-radisson-blu-dhaka, bd-pan-pacific-sonargaon-dhaka, bd-amari-dhaka, bd-dhaka-regency.

Modified existing published venues: 0. Unchanged existing venues: 0 (the starting repository had no dataset). Proposed deletions: none. Previously verified fields lost: none.

All eight have official-source business phone numbers. Six have published capacities with named seating layouts. Four have source-checked coordinates. None has independently corroborated verification status or reusable numerical ratings. Every record includes evidence URLs and dates. No venue photographs are republished.

Conflicts: Radisson's meeting summary says 1,100 people but Grand Ballroom theatre description says 1,050. Show 1,050 with an explicit caveat. Sheraton headline 1,000 is reception capacity; use 600 banquet seats. Do not compare unlike seating configurations as equivalent. Sonargaon's aggregate 2,500 headline is not admitted as a specific hall capacity.

Missing information: community-center coverage, most requested Dhaka neighborhoods, independent corroboration, four coordinate pairs, two capacities, several facilities and Sonargaon's neighborhood. Existing data was not invented to fill gaps. Exact official names are preserved; Bengali source aliases are retained in data but never used as interface labels.

Validation failures: none after correction and revalidation. See docs/QA.md for executed checks. This is a small hotel-focused collection, not a completed comprehensive Dhaka database. Further research remains necessary to meet the intended breadth.
