# Nearby control, mobile card fix and wedding-venue research — 27 October 2026

This release adds seven individually researched Dhaka event venues and makes the existing nearby-neighborhood filter available directly below the area chips on phones. It also removes the mobile card badge's absolute positioning so long venue names and area labels cannot cover addresses. Existing venue records, saved data, comparison, map and URL behavior are preserved.

## Sources and admission decisions

The owner's [China Land Maps link](https://maps.app.goo.gl/5qx4uftWwQtCrMdF8) was opened as the named business profile. The owner reports that weddings are held there; the listing independently confirms the party-centre identity, address, map point and public number. The owner report is retained as an untested wedding-use statement rather than a booking guarantee. [Haque's venue-branded page](https://sites.google.com/view/haquesbanquethallrestaurantur/home) explicitly offers wedding bookings and publishes its address and three business hotlines; its primary line and premises match the independently inspected business profile. Other exact profile URLs, review dates and field-level evidence are attached to the individual JSON records. No ratings, review text, photos, opening hours or Google Places bulk content were stored.

The existing checksum-verified 25 October Geofabrik/OSM extract was reused locally to check Modhumilon's previously mapped name, street number and point. OSM attribution and ODbL-1.0 terms remain in the website. One [Nominatim reverse lookup](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/reverse?lat=23.7304015&lon=90.4121767&format=jsonv2&addressdetails=1&zoom=18&accept-language=en) supported Bird's Eye's broad Paltan browsing locality. The map-based localities are not surveyed borders. All new numbers are public business contacts, normalized and source-qualified, not call-tested lines.

## Held or unresolved

- **Golden Gate Restaurant & Party Center, Khilgaon:** its marker is about 17 m from already indexed Blue Moon on Shahid Baki Road. Separate operator and premises identity has not been established, so adding it now risks a duplicate.
- **Rodevu Restaurant & Party Center, Khilgaon:** an older wedding booking page exists, but the matched business listing at 406/A Khilgaon is marked permanently closed. It was not added.
- **Block G, South Banasree:** generic locality name and map category do not establish an independent bookable venue. No specific operator contact was found.
- **SAQI Restaurant and Party Centre, Basabo:** older OSM point lacks a uniquely matched current profile in the targeted inspection.
- **Modhumilon:** no public venue phone in the checked map profile or OSM tags. Its exact street number still needs operator confirmation.
- **ALKADERIA and Bird's Eye websites:** the linked sites returned HTTP 403 and HTTP 503, respectively, through the available public route. Their content was not collected, and they were not marked as verified official websites.

The mobile nearby shortcut is contextual: it appears only after selecting a neighborhood with linked nearby areas, follows the same existing filter state and shareable URL, and disappears for All Dhaka. On narrow cards, the area badge now occupies normal layout space; if a long name leaves no room, the badge wraps before the address rather than overlaying it. The focused browser regression checks this at 320 CSS pixels and confirms that the shortcut updates both results and the URL. Full test and deployment results are recorded in the release workflow.
